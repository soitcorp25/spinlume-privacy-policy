Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Applies to the Spinlume app for Android

The short version

  • Spinlume has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress (levels, worlds, Daily challenge history, saved Skips and your settings, including your language) is stored on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

Spinlume ("the app", "we", "us") is a one-tap arcade game for Android phones. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at soitcorp25@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app stores a small amount of game data locally on your phone:

  • your current level and best level, which world you are in, the levels you cleared perfectly and the levels you skipped;
  • your Daily challenge history (the dates you completed the Daily, which world each one was in, and days you kept with a rewarded ad);
  • rewards you have earned, such as free Skips, and how many Skips you used today;
  • which new game mechanics you have already seen, so their explanation is shown only once;
  • your settings: Sound, Vibration, the Daily reminder (on or off, and its time) and the app language. If you choose a language in the app, Android stores that choice on your phone, and on Android 13 and later it also appears in your phone's language settings;
  • game counters used to pace ads and difficulty (for example, failures on the current level and levels completed since the last ad).

This data is not sent to us. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect analytics.

Android backup

If you have Google backup turned on for your phone, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google Account, so your progress can be restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spinlume is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one: for example to continue after a failed level or boss phase, to skip a level you are stuck on, or to keep your Daily run;
  • Interstitial ads, full-screen ads shown occasionally between levels.

To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process information from your device, such as:

DataPurpose
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, with consent where required, personalized ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, regional rules
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device, diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video completion)Ad measurement, rewards, fraud prevention

Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address from Google; we only see aggregated reports.

Your choices about ads

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you opt out of personalization, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are used. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.

For users in these regions, the legal bases for processing are your consent (personalized ads and related storage on your device) and our legitimate interest in keeping the app free, working and secure (showing non-personalized ads and preventing fraud).

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for short vibrations during play (for example, when a level fails), only if Vibration is on in Settings.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later asks you first): only for the optional Daily reminder, at most one a day at the time you choose, and only if you haven't played that day's Daily. The reminder is created on your phone; no data is sent to us or to a server for it. You can turn it off in Settings → Daily reminder or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Run at startup: so a Daily reminder you turned on still arrives after your phone restarts.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

6. Children

Spinlume is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information collected by Google AdMob is processed by Google as explained in section 3. Depending on where you live (for example, certain US states), the use of advertising identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" under local law; you can limit it with your phone's ad settings described above.

8. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app (and in your Google backup until it expires or you delete it). Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Security

We keep data collection to a minimum. Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections.

10. International transfers

Google may process ad-related information on servers in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own systems, most of these rights are exercised through your device settings, the in-app Privacy options, or directly with Google (Google data & privacy). You can also contact us and we will help. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how your information is used, we will show a notice in the app or in its store listing.
